\section*{SELECTED RESEARCH}
\begin{itemize}

\item \textbf{Data Assimilation and Uncertainty Propagation}: Built observation-guided workflows for sparse measurements and distribution shift; M.S. research quantified how atmospheric-density uncertainty drives orbital-state error growth.
\item \textbf{Continuous-Time and Generative Dynamics} \link{https://haoyangjiang-wm.github.io/neural-equation-demos/}{[Demos]}: Investigate learned flow maps, consistency, and flow matching; implement Neural ODE, learned-delay DDE, and integral models with differentiable solvers, adjoint methods, and long-horizon evaluation.
