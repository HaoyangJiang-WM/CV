## Research

- **Physical-system forecasting:** graph-based learning for directional transport, missing boundary context, river networks, and coastal dynamics.
- **Neural operators:** integral-equation formulations for boundary-value problems and incomplete physical domains.
- **Generative inverse modeling:** pretrained priors, inference-time optimization, full-waveform inversion, and second-order flow matching.
- **Continuous-time learning:** Neural ODE, learned-delay DDE, and neural integral models; adjoints, differentiable solvers, and long-horizon evaluation.
- **Space systems:** satellite trajectory modeling and orbital-error propagation under atmospheric-density uncertainty.
